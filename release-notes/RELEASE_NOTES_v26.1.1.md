# ChargeState v26.1.1

A bug-fix release: Open PDF works for drafts, and the buttons explain themselves.

## Bug fixes

- **Open PDF** now opens the draft PDF for a quote that has not been sent (it opens the sent PDF
  once there is one). Before, it stayed grayed out until a quote was sent, even right after
  **Draft PDF**. **Spreadsheet** works the same way.
- **Draft PDF** is now offered only for drafts. **Draft PDF**, **Open PDF**, **Spreadsheet**,
  **Sync now** and **Reload** explain what they do when you hover over them.
